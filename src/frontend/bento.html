operating principles

Reliability, by design.

Four rules I apply to every platform I own. They're how the numbers on the left actually happen.

  1. 01
    Infrastructure as code, no exceptions.

    Every resource lives in Bicep, reviewed in pull requests, deployed through pipelines. Portal changes get reverted.

  2. 02
    Automate the second time.

    Manual once is fine. Manual twice becomes a runbook. Manual three times becomes a script with tests.

  3. 03
    Observability before alerting.

    Dashboards, structured logs, and SLOs ship with the service. Alerts come last, and only on customer-visible symptoms.

  4. 04
    Blameless retros, durable fixes.

    Every incident produces an action item with an owner and a date. Nothing closes until the regression test exists.
